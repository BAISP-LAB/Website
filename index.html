實驗室簡介

Laboratory Introduction
中文介紹

我們的實驗室專注於 智慧生醫技術 的開發，特別著重於神經訊號與醫學影像的分析工具。 我們結合腦波（EEG）、功能性磁振造影（fMRI）、 心電圖（ECG）等生醫訊號與人工智慧技術， 目標是研發可應用於臨床疾病治療、監測與預防， 並提升日常生活品質的創新解決方案。

目前研究項目涵蓋 精準神經調控 、 駕駛安全 與 心血管手術後腦功能評估 等領域。除了運用機器學習與深度學習進行分類及預測， 我們也重視模型可解釋性，以建立神經生理標記， 深化對腦科學的理解，並推動智慧醫療發展。

English Introduction

Our laboratory focuses on the development of intelligent biomedical technologies , particularly tools for analyzing neurophysiological signals and medical imaging. We integrate electroencephalography (EEG), functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI), electrocardiography (ECG), and artificial intelligence to develop innovative solutions for disease treatment, monitoring, prevention, and improved quality of life.

Our research covers precision neuromodulation , driving safety , and postoperative brain-function assessment . In addition to machine-learning and deep-learning methods for classification and prediction, we emphasize model interpretability to identify neurophysiological markers, advance brain-science research, and promote intelligent healthcare.
